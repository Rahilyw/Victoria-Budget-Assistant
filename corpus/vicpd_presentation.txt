     policing.

     specialized resources.

     complex environment.

[VicPD 2026 Budget Presentation, slide 17]
Traffic – Current Duties
      •   Supports patrol by managing traffic calls
      •   Manages ASD fleet and calibrations
      •   Reviews impaired-driving investigations and IRPs
      •   Leads scheduled roadblocks
      •   Responds to community traffic concerns
      •   Leads Greater Victoria Motorcycle Escort Team training
      •   Plans and coordinates motorcycle escorts for public events
      •   Provides 24/7 on-call Collision Analysts and collaborates with MCU
      •   Investigates hit-and-run, parking, and abandoned-vehicle complaints
      •   Works with municipal bylaw and engineering staff on traffic-control issues
      •   Attends and prepares for Traffic Court
      •   Leads VicPDimplementation of e-ticketing technology
      •   Manages towing contract, reviews towing logs, and processes invoices
      •   Processes all Notices/Orders, IRPs, ADPs, prohibitions, and violation
          tickets
      •   Responds to FOI requests related to traffic enforcement
